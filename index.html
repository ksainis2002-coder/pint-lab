How it stays creamy without binders

Xanthan gumGuar gumPudding mixGelatinCornstarch

Sugar

Dissolved sugar lowers the freezing point, so part of the pint stays soft. Full-cream bases here keep 60 to 80 g. Cut it and the pint spins up crumbly. In protein pints, allulose softens much like sugar; erythritol freezes harder and can turn gritty.

Fat

Cream coats ice crystals and keeps them small. The richest copies use close to a 1:1 milk to cream ratio, which is what the store pints do with eggs and gums.

Protein

Skyr, Greek yogurt, ultra-filtered milk and protein powder add body to lighter pints. They spin up powdery the first time, which is normal: a splash of milk and a re-spin fixes it.

Technique

Freeze for a full 24 hours on a level shelf. Let the pint sit 5 minutes (10 for protein), spin, then re-spin with 1 tbsp of liquid if it looks dry. Leftovers: smooth the top flat, refreeze, and re-spin before eating.

Your tub size

The app starts at 450 ml, the Johnson Velvet Creme tub. 473 is the Ninja Creami pint and 709 the Creami Deluxe. Every recipe scales to the number you enter. Not sure of yours? Lean versions

For a cut, each full-cream recipe gets a lean twin: cream swaps to ultra-filtered milk and skyr, sugar to allulose, sauces to sugar-free, peanut butter to powdered, and the richest mix-ins are halved. Most land at 350 to 600 kcal and under 20 g fat per tub with 40 g+ protein.

Slushies and Italian ice

These are fat-free already, so their lean version is the same recipe. They need sugar to work: about 20 g per tub at least. Zero-sugar drinks and stevia freeze into a solid block that won't slush. Fizzy drinks: let the bubbles settle before freezing.

Swaps that work

Stevia drops can replace allulose: about 5 drops per tablespoon, but it does nothing for texture, so the tub freezes firmer and needs an extra re-spin. Greek yogurt can replace skyr or quark gram for gram. Use a thick, strained Greek style; regular runny yogurt has more water and less protein and turns icy.

Cream cheese, the one binder

One tablespoon of light cream cheese in a protein pint makes it smoother on the first spin and cuts down re-spins. The cheesecake flavours use 60 to 100 g as the main body. Soften it and blend it with the sugar first so it leaves no flecks. Use the Hide cream cheese button if you want pints without it.

Read one label

Many protein powders contain gums. For a fully binder-free protein pint, pick one whose label lists only protein, flavoring and sweetener. Store-bought mix-ins like cookies and candy are fine; it's the base that matters.
